# Getting started

## Connect your bank

1. Open **Bank connections** from the menu.
2. Pick your banks and continue. Your bank's sign in opens in a new tab.
3. Sign in and approve access. You'll then be shown an access code.
4. Paste the code into FinTrack.

You can pick more than one bank in step 2, but each one is approved on its own, with its own access code.

## What happens next

FinTrack syncs straight away, and your accounts and transactions appear once it's done.

After that it syncs every 4 hours, or press **Sync now** at the top of the page.

Each sync also:

- **Applies your rules** to new transactions.
- **Matches transfers** between your own accounts, so they don't count as spending. Any it isn't sure of appear under
  **To review**.
- **Adds bills** from your standing orders and direct debits, and matches payments to them.
- **Suggests subscriptions** from payments that repeat. These also appear under **To review**.
- **Spots your pay** once three similar payments have come in, so budgets can follow your pay day.
- **Records your balances** for net worth, working back through your history for new accounts.
- **Sends notifications** for anything you've asked to hear about, such as late bills or low balances.

## Keeping it connected

Each bank's access runs out after a while. FinTrack reminds you two weeks before, and shows a count next to **Bank
connections**. Renewing is the same sign in and access code.
